Version C · architecture

Mission Control — the play is the centre

You declare an outcome.
The system decides who does each step.
What a play is
A versioned file: goal, steps, who may own each, guardrails, done-conditions. Reviewable in a pull request, improvable after every run.
Why it fits seven people
Coordination stops being a meeting. Each person opens one inbox of steps already scoped, briefed and sequenced against everyone else's.
What you accept
You are formalising a process you have not run yet. Written too early, plays encode guesses and the team routes around them.
