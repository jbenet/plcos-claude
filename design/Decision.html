Choosing

Pick B. Ship A's screen in week three.

The three designs are not really equal-weight alternatives, and it would be evasive to present them that way. A alone means rebuilding in a year and losing the history — Attio mutates records, so replay is impossible and the learning loop never exists. C alone formalises a process you have not run yet, which encodes guesses the team then routes around. B's event log is the one thing that is cheap to start now and impossible to backfill later.

Whichever you pick · week one
About a week of work. None of it is wasted under any of the three designs.
01
Master relationship table
One row per relationship: owner, exposure across vehicles, scores, decision architecture, warm paths.
02
The ask log
Date, vehicle, asker, outcome. The highest-value table you will build, because it is the one that stops four raises colliding.
03
Meeting capture
Local-first transcription with a real DPA, flowing into the relationship record. Ends the “what did we tell them in June” problem.
04
Warm-path map
The existing-relationship census, with connector goodwill scored from memory before the first new ask goes out.
05
Start writing events
Append-only, from day one, even with no UI over it. First-touch and ask history are free today and unreconstructable in 2028.
