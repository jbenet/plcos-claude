One flag, worth acting on
Tessera is the only 506(b) vehicle while four others are generally soliciting.
The 2020 adopting release is explicit that an issuer may not run a 506(c) general solicitation in order to identify investors for a 506(b) offering. With an overlapping LP base and a visible public raise, demonstrating that each Tessera investor came from a pre-existing substantive relationship is an evidentiary burden that is easy to lose.
Two of Tessera's four investors also appear in Neurotech I pipeline touched by public content. Either convert Tessera to 506(c), or wall it off with documented relationship provenance per investor.
