Three ways to build
the fundraising system

All three cover the same ground: one relationship base, several vehicles, a team of seven-plus people each running agents, and a hard date. They differ in what sits at the centre — the CRM record, the graph, or the play. That choice determines the build cost, the ceiling, and what the software can eventually learn.
