Where the
intelligence goes
Agents do the reading
Research, enrichment, monitoring, summarising, first drafts, retrieval. Everything with a citation trail a person can check in seconds.
People do the judging
Every conversation, every ask, every concession, every decision about who to approach and when. No agent contacts a counterparty.
The system does the remembering
Who asked whom for what, when, and what came back — across seven people, four vehicles and three years.
