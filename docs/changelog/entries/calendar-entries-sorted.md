# Travel and Events sorted from your own calendar, and only LP meetings · 10 Oct 2026

Issue 0021 follow-up. Nobody has to keep a separate travel calendar. The Calendar page reads every calendar each
person's mailguard token can read, GET only, at most hourly, and sorts each entry. A conference, summit or talk, or
an entry with a Luma, Eventbrite or registration link, is an Event. An entry over two or more days with a place is
Travel, and the place is the destination. Everything else is a meeting and stays next to the LP. To say otherwise,
put [travel] or [event] in the title, give it the colour picked for Travel or Events in Preferences, or click
Travel, Event or Meeting under it on the Calendar page; the click is remembered. A pasted calendar address, such as
TripIt's, is still read, and its entries are sorted the same way. If a token cannot read calendars, the page says
why in words.

Meetings next to LPs now count only LP meetings. One with no guests, only our own people, or nobody of ours leaves
nothing. An outside guest makes it an LP's when their address is the LP's or their email domain is one we hold for
the LP's firm. Only when no guest matched does an LP's name in the entry count; such a meeting says it was matched by
name, with lower confidence, and "Not this LP" on the LP's timeline removes it for good. Vendors, recruiters and
portfolio companies leave nothing.
