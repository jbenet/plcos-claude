# Meetings from the calendar, read only · 8 Oct 2026

Issue 0021. Juan approved reading calendars, read only, so meetings show next to the LPs they were with. Each
person's mailguard key now also reads their Google Calendar when its tool holds `calendar.read`. A meeting with
someone on record lands as a touchpoint for that LP and their firm. It shows on the LP's page, in the comms trace
as "Google Calendar, via mailguard", and in reconciliation as evidence. It keeps the date and who on the team was
there, never the title or the guests' names. Affinity's copy of the same meeting wins.

Nothing is written to a calendar and no invitation is answered. A key that can invite people or answer
invitations is refused. It reads daily after Affinity; Preferences → Email shows the state and has "Read calendars
now". It needs mailguard v0.9, which brings the calendar. Until then the card says the calendar is not available
yet. Details are in docs/25 §13.
