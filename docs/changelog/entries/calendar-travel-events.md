# Travel and Events on the Calendar · 8 Oct 2026

Issue 0021. Juan asked to see team travel and the events the team attends on the Calendar page, read from the
team's Google Calendars, and approved reading calendars read only. Each person pastes a calendar's private iCal
address in Preferences → Email → Calendars and picks Travel or Events. The all-vehicles Calendar then shows two
new lanes, with whose calendar each trip or event came from. An event two people attend shows once, with both.

The addresses are kept encrypted and shown back only masked. Only https addresses at Google Calendar, TripIt,
Outlook or iCloud are read, at most hourly, and nothing is ever written to a calendar. Until someone adds one,
the Calendar page says where to paste them. Details are in docs/25 §14.
