# A reply is owed only for an unanswered message · 7 Oct 2026

JuanMail counted 41 Neurotech LPs and 3 Rails LPs as owed a reply, far more than its mail shows. The outreach queue
used the LP page's "they spoke last", which counts a meeting as theirs: every LP whose latest touch was a meeting read
as waiting on us.

Now `replyOwed` and the `reply_owed` bucket mean their latest email or message (not an automatic reply, not their
firm's mail) came after anything of ours: our own message, or a meeting or call together. The LP page is unchanged.
