# Meetings, calendar, fit and vehicle status, redesigned — issues 0072–0075

Juan found tonight's versions of these four pages hard to use. This pass puts his requests back on the earlier look —
the queue beside the brief, the grouped fit work-queue, the heat of the calendar, the vehicle table with its "why there
is no total" pane — and keeps tonight's data fixes: meetings and briefs scoped to the vehicle, fit read from each
pursuit's latest strategy for that vehicle (never a better score borrowed from another), the calendar's window on
today's date, and the batched close reads.

**Meetings (0074).** The queue is back on the left: coming up, date passed and not confirmed, then held, newest first,
25 at a time, with a search. The pane is what the next conversation needs, and points at the LP workspace for the rest:

- four cells at a glance: status and ladder, close, contact, provisional score;
- a short brief, label and a line or two each: where we left it, next step, why they'd care, three sourced claims,
  and how many claims it will not assert, with the full list one tap away;
- path to closing: the six statuses, the plan's next moves, each commitment's soft → signed → hard → closed → cash
  track, and the rung the meeting could justify;
- the four readings (tap for each basis; nothing hover-only), open questions and the latest contact.

The "what each held meeting justifies" list is gone. A held meeting shows what happened at the top of its own pane.
Generated briefs are still to come (0078).

**Calendar (0072).** The list is newest first, with a Newest/Oldest switch. It has LP and Our team columns, filters for
each, and the When, LP and Our team headers sort it; a row with no LP or team goes last either way. The LP's name no
longer repeats in the label. Where the list is narrow, as on an iPad with the side pane open, LP and team move under the
label. The week grid is now a heat grid that fits the page: a darker cell is a busier week, and this week is outlined.

**Fit (0073).** The earlier grouped layout is back, filled: strong, good, possible and weak fit, fit not known, fails a
gate and no reading yet. Each group carries the work it means. Each row shows its rank, score and whether it is assessed
or provisional, the reason, the four readings, the gates, the status and the owner, 30 to a page. The side pane counts
each group and filters by it. `/<vehicle>/fit/<lp>` shows the provisional reading where there is no formal assessment:
the verdict, the gates, the four readings with their bases, and what the strategy proposes.

**Vehicle status (0075).** The pursuits list that repeated Soft → Hard is gone. For a vehicle:

- the hard-only headline;
- the pipeline as counts by status, with meetings in the last 30 days and those coming up;
- the close cycle's open conditions, listed once;
- "In close": every committed LP or LP with money on file, with each commitment's five-step track, the next step and,
  one tap away, its open questions, conditions and notes;
- a close timeline of the recorded milestone dates on one axis against today and the close target. It says plainly that
  planned dates are not modelled yet.

All vehicles keeps the earlier table with no total row.

New styles are in CSS modules beside each page. `components/status/CloseSteps` is shared by meetings and status and
replaces `CloseProgress`. Checked in a browser at 1440, 1180 and 390 px on the demo. TypeScript, boundaries and all
properties pass.
