# Weaker indirect routes fold under a stronger direct one · 7 Oct 2026

Feedback 0131 and 0132: the route map and the comparison list showed long chains through connectors next to a
direct relationship that scored higher, which made the weaker chains look like real options.

Now, when an LP has a recommended direct route, every indirect route that scores below it (or has no score yet) is
shown as an alternative beneath that direct route instead of beside it. Nothing is removed: "Show alternatives" and the
expanded view still list every route, and a chain that scores higher than the direct route stays visible. Display
only; routes, scores and records are unchanged.
