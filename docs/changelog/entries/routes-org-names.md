# One map node for an organisation split across records · 7 Oct 2026

Feedback 0123: on the Netholabs route map one firm showed as three nodes, because its records are split across two
spellings of its name and a short form.

The route map now draws organisations with near-identical names as one node: the same letters once spacing,
punctuation and a legal form ("Inc.", "Ltd") are ignored, one typo apart, or a short form the other only adds a
generic word to ("Labs", "Capital", "Ventures"). The node says how many records it combines and its hover text lists
the names. Display only: the records stay unmerged, routes and scores are unchanged, and people are never combined by
name here. The thresholds and the word list are guesses from this one report.
