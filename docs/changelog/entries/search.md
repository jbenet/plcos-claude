# Search on Everyone and in every top bar · 8 Oct 2026

Network's lists (Everyone, People, Firms, LPs, Co-funders, Connectors) had only "Next 100" paging over about 114k
records. Each list now has a search box: the words match a name in order, on the server, and the cover line counts
the matches. Paging, the group tabs and the opened name keep the search.

Every page's top bar now has a search box. On a list that searches itself (Network's lists, Routes) a chip names the
list and the search runs there, GitHub style; Backspace in the empty box, or the chip's ×, drops the chip and
searches everything at `/search`: names on our records (first 100, with a link to the full Everyone search) and the
app's pages by title. The box hides on narrow screens.
