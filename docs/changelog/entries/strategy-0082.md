# Strategy 0082 — compare LP actions and whole-raise moves

The vehicle strategy page now shows ten compact “So next” entries. LP actions use shared Pipeline/Selection search and sort controls, with immediate filtering over the loaded corpus, fifty-row pages, visible evidence points and capital-per-team-hour estimates, and expandable action/evidence details. Missing monetary inputs stay unscored.

Strategic moves are typed, imported records with separate vehicle estimates and decisions. Their menu covers materials, public content, events, sourcing, enrichment, introductions, syndication and close operations. Each estimate is labelled GUESS with its basis, provenance, audience, cost and time to effect. The same incremental-capital-per-team-hour scale orders moves and LP actions; manual positions apply within that combined queue. Overlapping alternatives are never summed into a forecast or hard capital.

A person can choose, dismiss, restore or reorder a move with a reason. Transactions preserve decisions on reimport, reject stale edits and wrong vehicle scopes, and write an audit record. The page shows recent decisions. Choosing is a planning state, never send, introduction, spending or publication authorization. The append-only migration adds move and vehicle-scope records because sorting and commands require precise numeric inputs. Demo has twelve invented examples; private seeds remain outside git. Use **Import move menu** on the strategy page to load `data/real/strategy-moves/menu.json` inside the live server after integration.
