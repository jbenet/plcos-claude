# Selection as a table, organisations as rows, a warm intro box · issues 0091–0093

**Selection (0091).** The ranked list is now a table. Check size, routes and stage are aligned
columns, beside the LP, its score, meetings and last touch, and every column heading sorts. The
year tag is gone. The keyboard works on the table: up and down move the focus, `x` ticks the LP
in focus, and Enter opens it. A short hint says so on devices with a keyboard. In the side panel,
the buttons (open the strategy, select for an action, fit) now sit under the score, above the
long readings. On an iPad the stage column stays; only the rank number gives way.

**Pipeline (0092).** An organisation with several people pursued is now a row like any other,
with no grey band or folder. It has its own score, "best of N", which is its best person's score,
and it is ranked among the LPs by it. Its routes and meetings are added up. Its last touch and
their read are the latest and warmest. Its evidence is the furthest any of its people has got,
with that person named. Its people follow in smaller, indented rows on a thread, with only what
is their own: name, score, status, owner, next step, routes, meetings, last touch, read and
ladder. Each row opens its LP. An organisation pursued in its own right now gathers its people
pursued in the same vehicle, whichever name leads their rows. An organisation's LP page lists its
people on record, with the status and score of those pursued in the vehicle, and links to each.

**Why Juan's screenshots were misaligned.** The feedback box draws the page from the DOM
(`lib/capture.ts`, modern-screenshot). To stay fast on long pages, it leaves out elements below
the fold. A table kept its full on-page height, so the few rows left in the picture were
stretched to fill it: every row hundreds of pixels tall. It happened in 0067, 0083, 0089 and
0092. The capture now lets tables and their row groups take the height of what is drawn. This
was reproduced and the fix checked with the same redraw on the pipeline, which now matches a
real screenshot. Flags in the pipeline also open with a button rather than a disclosure element,
so a closed list is not in the page.

**LP page (0093).** A warm intro box sits above "Add context". It shows how many possible
connections the network holds for this LP: strong, promising, and weak or unscored. It lists the
three best routes with their scores, and a black "Find a Warm Intro" button opens this LP on the
routes page. The page now runs the routes page's own search (this vehicle's kind and scope), so
the two agree. On an iPad with the pane open, the two columns used to spill under the pane when
the main column had wide content (a close track). The side column now comes first instead.

Validation: typecheck and boundaries pass; 510 of 510 properties hold, one of them new. Checked
in Chromium on the demo at 1440×900 and at iPad width 1180×820 with touch, including keyboard
movement, ticking and sorting on the selection page. Real-data volume was not re-checked on this
code: the live app serves master, and no copy was taken. No screenshots or real records are
included.
