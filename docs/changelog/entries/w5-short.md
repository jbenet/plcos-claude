# Strategies come out short: W5's length limits are enforced · 11 Oct 2026

Design A, step 3 of 3 (Juan, 11 Oct 2026: "set W5's length limits so new strategies come out short" — "SGTM").

- W5 1.13: why they'd care in **25 words**, the next step in **15** (who and when are their own fields), the
  route's reason in 15, up to **three risks of 8 words** each (the LP page shows them as chips), three open
  questions, each score's basis in 20 words and each fit reason in 25.
- A strategy written to 1.13 or later that runs over is **refused** — by the checker, the server's import and
  cloud W5 — with the field named ("too long: angle over 25 words"). Strategies written before stay as they are
  and are only counted, as before.
